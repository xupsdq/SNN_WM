```text

→ recurrence across successive inputs
```

After this core mechanism chain, retain two parallel outcome modules:

```text

```

This answers:

The two outcome modules constrain what retained states look like and when they influence later processing. They are not sequential causal steps, and neither substitutes for the core successor-reuse bridge. Subsection boundaries may differ from figure boundaries, but the core dependency order may not.
